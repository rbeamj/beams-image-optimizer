# Roadmap

This roadmap is directional, not a promise or a delivery schedule. Beams Image Optimizer will remain browser-local by default and keep its processing engine reusable independently of the interface.

## Current: Image optimization

- Format conversion, compression, resizing, quality controls, recipes, batch processing, and downloads
- Clear per-result settings and byte statistics

## Future: Photo Studio candidates

- Background removal and transparent backgrounds
- Background replacement and product-photo scenes
- Cropping, rotation, and straightening
- Brightness, contrast, exposure, and color correction
- Product-photo enhancement
- Before/after comparison and zoom
- Batch editing and reusable product-photo templates
- Additional export formats where browser support makes them practical
- Local AI inference where model licensing, commercial-use rights, redistribution terms, device support, and privacy can all be verified

No AI model, remote processing service, account system, or image-upload backend is included in the current alpha.
