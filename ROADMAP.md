# Roadmap

This roadmap is directional, not a promise or a delivery schedule. Beams Image Optimizer remains browser-local by default and keeps its processing engine reusable independently of the interface.

## Current: Quick Optimize

- Format conversion, compression, resizing, quality controls, recipes, batch processing, and downloads
- Clear per-result settings and byte statistics

## In progress: Photo Studio foundation

- Professional browser canvas with selectable layers, text, basic shapes, opacity, ordering, undo/redo, zoom, pan, rotate, flip, center-crop presets, and local export
- Non-destructive adjustment controls for exposure, contrast, saturation, and hue
- Preserve the original input; never silently change product details

## Next: Photo Studio core

- Freeform crop and straighten handles, filters, color-temperature/tint controls, shadows, and a clear layer inspector
- Text style controls, shape colors, alignment guides, duplicate/lock/hide layers, and project save/load
- Better before/after splitter and export framing for all composed layers

## Longer term: local product-photo tools

- Browser-local background removal and transparent backgrounds
- Background replacement, product-photo scenes, and manually controlled shadows
- Batch editing and reusable product-photo templates
- AI enhancements and optional local upscaling only after source, model-weight, commercial-use, redistribution, device-support, and privacy terms are independently verified
- Additional export formats where browser support makes them practical

No AI model, remote processing service, account system, or image-upload backend is included in the current alpha.
